How to Prepare Authentic Nigerian Jollof Rice

This is a simple guide on how to prepare authentic Nigerian Jollof Rice.

Key Cooking Terms

Parboil
To partially boil rice in water before completing the cooking process in the pepper sauce.
Reduction
The process of simmering liquid (such as blended tomatoes) to evaporate water and intensify flavor.
Smokiness
The distinct flavor achieved by allowing the bottom layer of rice to slightly brown at low heat at the end of cooking.

Required Ingredients

  • 3 cups long-grain parboiled rice
  • 6 large fresh tomatoes
  • 3 red bell peppers (Tatashe)
  • 2 Scotch bonnet peppers (Ado / Rodo)
  • 1 large red onion (half blended, half chopped)
  • 1/2 cup vegetable oil
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 2 cups chicken stock
  • 1 teaspoon curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon dried thyme
  • 2 bay leaves
  • Salt and seasoning cubes to taste

Safety and Preparation Warnings

Important Warning: When handling hot Scotch bonnet peppers, always wear gloves or wash your hands thoroughly with soap immediately after slicing. Avoid touching your eyes or face. Additionally, exercise extreme caution when opening the hot pot lid, as trapped steam can cause severe skin burns.

Step-by-Step Preparation Instructions

  1. Blend the fresh tomatoes, red bell peppers, Scotch bonnet peppers, and half of the onion until smooth.
  2. Pour the blended mixture into a pot and boil over medium heat until the excess water evaporates and the sauce thickens.
  3. In a separate large pot, heat the vegetable oil and fry the remaining chopped onions until translucent.
  4. Add the tomato paste to the oil and stir-fry for 3–5 minutes to reduce acidity.
  5. Pour in the reduced pepper mixture and stir continuously until the oil separates from the sauce.
  6. Add the chicken stock, curry powder, thyme, bay leaves, salt, and seasoning cubes. Cover and bring to a simmer.
  7. Rinse the parboiled rice thoroughly in cold water until the water runs clear, then stir it into the simmering sauce.
  8. Cover tightly with aluminum foil and a lid to trap steam, then lower the heat to low.
  9. Allow the rice to cook in the steam for about 25–30 minutes until soft and fluffy.
  10. Turn off the heat and let the rice rest for 5 minutes before stirring gently with a wooden spoon.
